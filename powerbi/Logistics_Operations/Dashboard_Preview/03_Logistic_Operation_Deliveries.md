## 🚚 Delivery Performance Analysis

### 🧾 Description
This section focuses on delivery efficiency by analysing delivery time distribution, on-time performance trends, and carrier effectiveness.

The goal is to identify operational bottlenecks, understand delay drivers, and evaluate how delivery duration and carrier performance impact overall service levels.

---

## 📌 Key Findings & Recommendations

### 1. Delivery performance is marginal
On-time delivery performance stands at **51%**, with late deliveries at **49%**, indicating widespread inefficiencies across the delivery process.

**Recommendation:**  
Introduce SLA tracking and standardised delivery benchmarks to improve consistency.

---

### 2. Longer delivery durations drive delays
Delivery time distribution shows a concentration of orders in higher delivery day ranges, with longer delivery durations strongly associated with late deliveries.

**Recommendation:**  
Optimise last-mile delivery and reduce average delivery time through better routing and fulfilment processes.

---

### 3. Performance improves over time but remains inconsistent
On-time performance shows an upward trend across months, but fluctuations indicate instability in operations.

**Recommendation:**  
Stabilise delivery processes by identifying peak disruption periods and improving capacity planning.

---

### 4. Carrier efficiency varies significantly
Carriers with higher order volumes do not always deliver better performance, with some high-volume carriers showing elevated late delivery rates.

**Recommendation:**  
Reduce reliance on underperforming carriers and rebalance volume towards more efficient partners.

---

### 5. Volume does not equal performance
Higher shipment volumes correlate with increased late delivery percentages, highlighting operational strain under demand.

**Recommendation:**  
Implement load balancing strategies across carriers and regions to prevent bottlenecks.

---

## 🧠 Executive Summary
Delivery performance is constrained by longer delivery durations, inconsistent carrier efficiency, and operational strain under high demand. While performance trends show improvement over time, systemic inefficiencies remain. Targeted optimisation of delivery times, carrier allocation, and process standardisation is critical to improving overall service levels.

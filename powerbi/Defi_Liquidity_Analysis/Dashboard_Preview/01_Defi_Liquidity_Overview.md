# DeFi Liquidity & Capital Flow Dashboard

## Overview

This page focuses on **current market conditions**, highlighting where liquidity is moving, which chains are gaining traction, and how capital is rotating across the DeFi ecosystem.

Unlike previous pages that establish macro relationships and structural trends, this view is designed to capture **short-term momentum and capital flows**.

---

## What This Page Shows

### 1. Market Activity KPIs

- **Total Chain TVL**  
  Overall size of the DeFi market across all chains.

- **7D Flow**  
  Net capital movement over the past 7 days, indicating whether liquidity is entering or leaving the ecosystem.

- **Positive Breadth %**  
  Percentage of protocols showing positive performance, providing a view of how widespread growth is.

- **Total TVL (Current)**  
  Latest total value locked, used as a reference for current market size.

---

### 2. 30-Day Liquidity Growth Trends

A multi-chain time series showing:
- TVL growth rates across major chains  
- Relative momentum between ecosystems  

This highlights which chains are accelerating or losing momentum over time.

---

### 3. TVL Distribution by Chain

A snapshot of liquidity concentration across chains:
- Identifies dominant ecosystems  
- Highlights relative scale differences  

---

### 4. Weekly Capital Movement Table

Displays:
- Chain-level **7D flows**
- Current **TVL levels**

Used to identify:
- Where capital is flowing into  
- Which chains are losing liquidity  

---

### 5. Liquidity Rotation Across Chains

A simplified trend view showing how liquidity shifts between chains across cycles.

---

## Key Insights

- **Liquidity Conditions Are Currently Weakening**  
  Negative 7-day flow suggests short-term capital outflows from the DeFi ecosystem.

- **Growth Is Not Broad-Based**  
  Low positive breadth indicates that only a small portion of protocols are driving performance, rather than widespread expansion.

- **Capital Is Selective, Not Uniform**  
  Flows are concentrated in specific chains, reinforcing that capital rotates rather than disperses evenly.

- **Momentum Varies Significantly Across Chains**  
  Some ecosystems show strong short-term growth, while others lag or decline, highlighting competitive dynamics.

- **Large Chains Maintain Structural Dominance**  
  Despite short-term flows, major chains continue to hold the majority of liquidity.

- **Short-Term Flows Differ From Long-Term Trends**  
  Even in a structurally growing ecosystem, short-term capital movement can be volatile and cyclical.

---

## What This Achieves

This page provides a **real-time view of DeFi market conditions**:

- Identifies **where capital is moving now**
- Distinguishes between **momentum and structural growth**
- Highlights **risk-on vs risk-off behaviour**
- Complements earlier analysis by adding a **tactical perspective**

---

## Summary

While the broader DeFi ecosystem continues to evolve structurally, short-term capital flows reveal a more dynamic picture. Liquidity is not evenly distributed but instead rotates selectively between chains, driven by relative opportunity, performance, and market conditions.

Understanding these flows is key to identifying where growth is occurring now, rather than where it has occurred historically.

# BTC–DeFi Correlation Analysis

## Overview

This page explores the relationship between Bitcoin price action and overall DeFi liquidity (Total Value Locked - TVL). The goal is to understand whether DeFi growth is driven by internal ecosystem adoption or broader crypto market conditions.

The dashboard combines time-series analysis, statistical correlation, and distribution visualisation to provide both a macro and structural view of DeFi liquidity behaviour.

---

## What This Page Shows

### 1. Total DeFi Liquidity
Displays the current total value locked across all chains, providing a snapshot of overall DeFi market size.

### 2. Correlation Metrics
- **Correlation BTC vs TVL:** Measures the strength of the relationship between Bitcoin price and DeFi liquidity over the full dataset.
- **Rolling 30-Day Correlation:** Captures how this relationship changes over time, highlighting periods of stronger or weaker alignment.

### 3. Time Series Comparison
A dual-axis line chart comparing:
- Bitcoin price (USD)
- Total DeFi TVL (USD)

This allows visual identification of cycles, expansions, and contractions across both markets.

### 4. Scatter Plot Analysis
A distribution of BTC price vs DeFi TVL:
- Each point represents a moment in time
- Colour-coded by year to show regime shifts
- Includes a trend line to highlight the overall relationship

---

## Key Insights

- **Strong Positive Relationship**  
  DeFi TVL and Bitcoin price show a clear positive correlation, indicating that liquidity expansion is closely tied to broader market conditions.

- **Market-Driven Liquidity Growth**  
  Rolling correlation suggests that DeFi growth is not fully independent. Instead, liquidity tends to expand during periods of overall crypto market strength.

- **TVL Follows Price Action**  
  In most cycles, TVL reacts to Bitcoin rather than leading it. This implies capital enters DeFi as part of wider risk-on behaviour.

- **Weak Independence During Corrections**  
  During Bitcoin drawdowns, DeFi does not yet demonstrate strong independent demand, suggesting limited decoupling from macro crypto trends.

- **Ethereum Influence on Correlation**  
  Given that the majority of DeFi liquidity is built on Ethereum, ETH price dynamics are likely a key driver behind the observed relationship.

---

## What This Achieves

This page establishes the **macro foundation** of the analysis:

- Confirms that DeFi liquidity is largely **market-driven**, not purely adoption-driven  
- Quantifies the relationship between **price and liquidity**  
- Identifies that DeFi currently behaves as a **high-beta extension of the crypto market**  
- Sets context for further analysis on **Ethereum dominance and chain-level liquidity rotation**

---

## Summary

DeFi does not currently operate as an isolated financial ecosystem. Instead, it expands and contracts alongside the broader crypto market, with Bitcoin acting as a primary driver of liquidity conditions.

Understanding this relationship is essential before analysing how liquidity is distributed across chains or where capital is rotating within the ecosystem.

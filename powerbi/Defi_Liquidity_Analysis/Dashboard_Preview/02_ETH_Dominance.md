# Ethereum Dominance vs Emerging Chains

## Overview

This page examines how DeFi liquidity is distributed between Ethereum and alternative chains, and how this distribution has evolved over time. 

The goal is to understand whether Ethereum continues to dominate the ecosystem or if liquidity is increasingly rotating into emerging chains.

## Key Insights

- **Ethereum Remains Dominant**  
  Ethereum continues to hold the majority of DeFi liquidity (~79%), confirming its role as the core infrastructure layer.

- **Non-Ethereum Share is Increasing**  
  The steady rise in non-ETH share suggests that liquidity is gradually diversifying across alternative ecosystems.

- **Growth is Uneven Across Chains**  
  Liquidity expansion is not evenly distributed — certain chains capture significantly more capital during growth phases.

- **Liquidity Rotates, Not Disperses**  
  Capital tends to rotate between chains rather than spread evenly, indicating competitive dynamics within the ecosystem.

- **Solana Shows Strong Cyclical Growth**  
  Solana demonstrates sharp expansions during bullish periods, suggesting high responsiveness to market conditions.

- **Base Emerging as a Growth Chain**  
  Base shows consistent upward momentum despite starting from a smaller base, indicating increasing adoption.

- **BSC Reflects Earlier Cycle Dominance**  
  BSC peaked earlier in previous cycles but shows comparatively weaker relative growth in recent periods.

- **Layer 2 Expansion is Increasingly Important**  
  Arbitrum and Base highlight the growing role of Ethereum scaling solutions in capturing liquidity.

## What This Achieves

This page provides a **structural view of the DeFi ecosystem**:

- Identifies whether liquidity is **concentrated or diversifying**
- Highlights **which chains are gaining traction over time**
- Demonstrates that DeFi growth is increasingly **multi-chain rather than Ethereum-only**
- Shows that capital movement is driven by **relative opportunity and performance across chains**

---

## Summary

While Ethereum remains the dominant platform, the DeFi ecosystem is gradually becoming more distributed. Liquidity is increasingly rotating into alternative chains, particularly during strong market conditions, indicating a shift toward a more competitive and multi-chain environment.

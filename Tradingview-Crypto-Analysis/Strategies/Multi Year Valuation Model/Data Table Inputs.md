## Valuation Framework

The valuation table combines three major analytical categories to assess Bitcoin’s long-term market positioning.

---

### On-Chain Metrics

These indicators evaluate **blockchain activity and investor behavior** to estimate underlying network value.

**Examples include:**

- MVRV  
- NUPL  
- CVDD  
- NVTS  
- BTC Thermocap  

These metrics help determine whether Bitcoin is **historically undervalued or overvalued relative to realized capital and network activity**.

---

### Price-Based Valuation Models

These indicators apply **statistical and financial analysis techniques** to measure risk-adjusted performance and price behavior.

**Examples include:**

- RSI valuation scaling  
- Sharpe Ratio  
- Sortino Ratio  
- Omega Ratio  
- Realized Price comparisons  

These models provide a **quantitative perspective on market momentum, volatility, and return efficiency**.

---

### Cycle & Behavioral Indicators

These models attempt to capture **long-term Bitcoin cycle dynamics**.

**Examples include:**

- Pi-Cycle Top Indicator  
- Meme Coin Cycle Excess  
- Risk Metrics  
- Power Law Trend Models  

These indicators provide context around **macro cycle positioning within Bitcoin’s historical relationship with the business market structure**.

# 📊 DeFi Liquidity & Market Structure Dashboard

## Overview

This project explores **DeFi liquidity, capital flows, and market structure** using on-chain data.

Rather than focusing purely on price, the dashboards analyse:

* **Total Value Locked (TVL)**
* **Capital rotation across chains**
* **Ethereum dominance**
* **Correlation between Bitcoin and DeFi liquidity**

Built using **Power BI**, this project demonstrates how blockchain data can be transformed into **quantitative insights and market analysis**.

---

## 📁 Dashboard Structure

The analysis is split into three core views:

---

### 🔹 01. DeFi Liquidity Overview

**Files:**

* `01_Defi_Liquidity_Overview.png`
* `01_Defi_Liquidity_Overview.md`

**Focus:**

* Total DeFi TVL
* Weekly capital flows
* Liquidity distribution across chains

**Key Insight:**

* Liquidity is **not static** — capital continuously rotates between ecosystems
* Short-term flows highlight **risk-on / risk-off behaviour**

---

### 🔹 02. Ethereum Dominance & Market Structure

**Files:**

* `02_ETH_Dominance.png`
* `02_ETH_Dominance.md`

**Focus:**

* Ethereum vs non-Ethereum liquidity share
* TVL distribution across chains
* Growth of alternative ecosystems

**Key Insight:**

* Ethereum remains the **core liquidity hub (~80% dominance)**
* Non-ETH ecosystems are growing but remain **secondary**
* Market structure is **gradually diversifying**

---

### 🔹 03. BTC vs DeFi Liquidity Correlation

**Files:**

* `03_BTC_vs_DEFI.png`
* `03_BTC_vs_Defi.md`

**Focus:**

* Relationship between Bitcoin price and DeFi TVL
* Correlation and rolling correlation analysis
* Market dependency on macro crypto trends

**Key Insight:**

* Strong positive correlation (~0.85) between BTC and DeFi liquidity
* DeFi expansion is largely driven by **broader market strength**, not isolated growth
* TVL tends to **follow BTC**, rather than lead it

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

* **Liquidity is highly dynamic**, rotating across chains rather than remaining fixed
* **Ethereum dominates DeFi**, acting as the primary liquidity hub
* **Alternative chains grow in cycles**, often driven by narratives and incentives
* **DeFi is strongly correlated with BTC**, indicating reliance on macro market conditions
* Market behaviour is driven by **capital flow, not just price action**

# 📊 Logistics Operations Dashboard

## 🧾 Description
End-to-end analysis of logistics performance across regions, product categories, and time using SQL and Power BI.

The dashboard is designed to identify delivery inefficiencies, highlight operational bottlenecks, and support data-driven decision-making in high-volume logistics environments.

---

## 📌 Key Findings & Recommendations

### 1. System-wide inefficiency
On-time performance remains marginal at **51%**, with late deliveries at **49%**, indicating delays are widespread rather than isolated.

**Recommendation:**  
Standardise delivery processes and introduce SLA tracking to reduce variability across operations.

---

### 2. Regional performance imbalance
The **North region** handles the highest order volume but also records the highest delays, suggesting capacity constraints under scale.

**Recommendation:**  
Reallocate logistics capacity or optimise routing strategies in high-volume regions.

---

### 3. Volume vs efficiency gap
Higher order volumes do not translate into better delivery performance, highlighting operational strain in peak demand areas.

**Recommendation:**  
Implement load balancing across carriers and regions to improve delivery consistency.

---

### 4. Category-driven delays
**Clothing and Electronics** contribute the most to both order volume and delays, making them key drivers of inefficiency.

**Recommendation:**  
Prioritise these categories for fulfilment optimisation and inventory positioning.

---

### 5. Positive delivery trend
On-time delivery performance improves steadily throughout the year, peaking in later months, suggesting operational improvements or seasonal efficiency gains.

**Recommendation:**  
Analyse successful periods to replicate best practices across earlier months.

---

### 6. Returns vs delivery issues
Return volumes remain relatively low compared to delivery delays, indicating that **timeliness is a bigger issue than product quality**.

**Recommendation:**  
Focus operational improvements on delivery speed and reliability rather than returns handling.

---

## 🧠 Executive Summary
Delivery performance is constrained by systemic inefficiencies, with high-volume regions and product categories driving delays despite improving trends over time. Targeted optimisation of logistics capacity, routing, and high-impact categories presents the biggest opportunity for performance improvement.

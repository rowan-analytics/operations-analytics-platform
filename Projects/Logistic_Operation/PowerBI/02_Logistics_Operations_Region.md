## 🌍 Regional Analysis

### 🧾 Description
This section analyses logistics performance across regions, focusing on order volume, customer segmentation, carrier efficiency, and delay distribution.

The goal is to identify regional bottlenecks and understand how demand, delivery performance, and carrier activity vary across locations.

---

## 📌 Key Findings & Recommendations

### 1. North region drives both volume and delays
The **North region** handles the highest order volume while also recording the highest late deliveries, indicating performance issues under scale.

**Recommendation:**  
Increase logistics capacity and optimise delivery routing in high-demand regions.

---

### 2. Customer demand is retail-driven
**Retail customers consistently generate the highest order volume** across all regions, making them the primary driver of logistics demand.

**Recommendation:**  
Align fulfilment and delivery resources with retail-heavy demand patterns.

---

### 3. Regional imbalance in performance
Lower-volume regions such as **West and South show reduced delays**, suggesting better operational control at smaller scale.

**Recommendation:**  
Apply operational strategies from lower-volume regions to improve performance in high-volume areas.

---

### 4. Carrier performance varies by region
Certain carriers dominate order volume but do not consistently deliver better performance across regions.

**Recommendation:**  
Rebalance carrier allocation based on both volume and delivery efficiency.

---

### 5. Delay distribution highlights regional inefficiencies
Late deliveries are concentrated in high-demand regions, reinforcing that **delivery duration and scale are key drivers of delays**.

**Recommendation:**  
Optimise last-mile delivery and reduce delivery times in delay-heavy regions.

---

## 🧠 Executive Summary
Regional performance is uneven, with high-volume areas such as the North experiencing the greatest delivery challenges. Retail demand is the dominant driver of order volume, while carrier efficiency and delivery duration play a critical role in determining regional performance. Targeted optimisation of high-demand regions offers the largest opportunity for improvement.

# 📊 Logistics Operations Dashboard (Power BI)

## Overview

This dashboard provides an **end-to-end view of logistics performance**, focusing on delivery efficiency, regional performance, and operational bottlenecks.

The analysis moves beyond simple KPI tracking to identify **why delays occur and where operational improvements can be made**.

Built using **Power BI**, this dashboard is designed for **operations teams, logistics managers, and performance analysts**.

---

## 📁 Dashboard Structure

The dashboard is divided into three key sections:

---

### 🔹 01. Operations Overview

**Files:**

* `01_Logistics_Operations_Overview.png`
* `01_Logistic_Operations_Description.md`

**Focus:**

* High-level KPIs
* Overall delivery performance
* System-wide efficiency

**Key Metrics:**

* On-time delivery rate
* Late delivery percentage
* Total deliveries

**Insight:**
Provides a **top-level operational snapshot**, helping quickly identify whether performance issues are widespread or isolated.

---

### 🔹 02. Regional Performance Analysis

**Files:**

* `02_Logistics_Operations_Region.png`
* `02_Logistic_Operations_Region.md`

**Focus:**

* Performance by region
* Delivery success rates across locations
* Identification of underperforming areas

**Insight:**
Highlights **regional inefficiencies**, allowing teams to:

* identify problem regions
* compare performance across locations
* allocate resources more effectively

---

### 🔹 03. Delivery Performance Analysis

**Files:**

* `03_Logistic_Operations_Delivery.png`
* `03_Logistic_Operations_Delivery.md`

**Focus:**

* Delivery time distribution
* Drivers of delays
* Relationship between delivery duration and performance

**Insight:**
Shows that **longer delivery times are a key driver of late deliveries**, helping pinpoint operational bottlenecks.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

* Nearly **half of deliveries are late (~49%)**, indicating systemic inefficiencies
* Certain regions consistently underperform, suggesting **location-specific issues**
* **Delivery duration is strongly linked to delays**, highlighting process inefficiencies
* High-volume operations do not always correlate with better performance

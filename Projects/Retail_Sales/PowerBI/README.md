# 📊 Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

## Overview

This project presents a **comprehensive retail sales analytics dashboard**, analysing performance across:

* revenue trends
* customer behaviour
* product performance
* geographic sales distribution

The goal is to move beyond basic reporting and provide **clear, business-focused insights** that support decision-making in an **e-commerce / marketplace environment**.

Built using **Power BI**, this project reflects real-world analytics workflows used in companies such as **Shopee and Lazada**.

---

## 📁 Dashboard Structure

The dashboard is divided into four key analytical views:

---

### 🔹 01. Executive Overview

**Files:**

* `01_Executive_Overview.png`
* `01_Executive_Overview.md`

**Focus:**

* High-level KPIs
* Monthly revenue trends
* Top products and customers

**Key Insight:**

* Revenue shows **strong growth toward year-end**, indicating seasonal demand
* Performance is driven by a **small number of high-impact products and customers**

---

### 🔹 02. Customer Insights

**Files:**

* `02_Customer_Insights.png`
* `02_Customer_Insights.md`

**Focus:**

* Customer revenue distribution
* Order behaviour
* High-value customer identification

**Key Insight:**

* Revenue is **highly concentrated**, with top customers contributing a large share
* Customer value varies significantly beyond order frequency

---

### 🔹 03. Product Analysis

**Files:**

* `03_Product_Analysis.png`
* `03_Product_Analysis.md`

**Focus:**

* Revenue by product
* Units sold vs revenue
* Product performance distribution

**Key Insight:**

* A small number of products drive **disproportionate revenue**
* High volume does not always equal high value

---

### 🔹 04. Sales Performance (Geographic)

**Files:**

* `04_Sales_Performance.png`
* `04_Sales_Performance.md`

**Focus:**

* Revenue by country
* Order distribution across regions
* Market performance comparison

**Key Insight:**

* Revenue is **heavily concentrated in the UK**, with other markets contributing less
* Clear opportunity for **international growth and expansion**

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

* **Revenue is concentrated** across customers, products, and geography
* **Top customers and products drive performance**, creating dependency risk
* **Seasonality plays a major role** in revenue growth
* **Market expansion opportunities exist** outside the core region
* **Volume and value are not always aligned**, highlighting importance of pricing and product mix

## 📍 Positioning & Forward Outlook

Current positioning reflects a focus on **accumulating long-term high-conviction assets within a perceived value zone**, rather than maximising short-term directional exposure. Approximately **35% of capital is deployed**, with the **majority held in cash**, allowing for continued participation while preserving flexibility in an environment that remains reactive and selective.

---

### 🎯 Opportunity Set

The current opportunity set appears increasingly **selective**, with stronger performance concentrated in specific assets and themes rather than broad-based market strength. This suggests that returns are being driven more by **relative strength and capital concentration** than by general market beta, favouring targeted exposure over indiscriminate participation.

---

### ⚠️ Key Risks

Key risks remain linked to ongoing geopolitical developments and headline-driven volatility, which may result in **sharp but short-lived price movements without sustained follow-through**. Elevated cross-asset correlations also present a risk, potentially reducing diversification benefits and increasing the impact of broader market weakness.

---

### 🔁 Forward Plan

Going forward, portfolio exposure will be adjusted dynamically based on changes in market structure and participation.

* If **trend strength and market breadth improve**, capital will be deployed more aggressively to increase directional exposure.
* If **momentum weakens or volatility rises without sustained follow-through**, exposure will be reduced in favour of capital preservation and maintaining liquidity.

---

### 🧠 Positioning Framework

This approach reflects a preference for **adaptive positioning**, ensuring that capital is aligned with evolving market conditions rather than fixed to a static directional view.

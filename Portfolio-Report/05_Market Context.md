## 🌍 Market Context

The market environment over the period was shaped by macro and geopolitical developments, with ongoing tensions contributing to **elevated, headline-driven volatility**. Price action became increasingly reactive to news flow, particularly during the early phase of the month, resulting in short-term dislocations and weaker trend continuity.

---

### 📉 Market Structure

From a structural perspective, the period can be divided into **two distinct phases**. The initial phase was characterised by weaker and more volatile price action, with inconsistent follow-through and reduced directional conviction. This transitioned into a more constructive environment, where trend strength improved and price action stabilised, supporting higher-quality directional opportunities.

---

### 🔗 Liquidity & Participation

Market participation remained **uneven** throughout much of the period, with performance concentrated in a narrower subset of assets rather than broad-based strength. This suggests capital flows were selective rather than systemic, reducing the effectiveness of passive exposure and increasing the importance of **asset selection and timing**.

As conditions improved later in the month, participation became more aligned with directional trends, enabling more efficient return generation.

---

### 🧠 Implications for Positioning

These dynamics reinforced the importance of **disciplined exposure and flexibility**. During the early phase, weaker structure and elevated volatility supported a more defensive positioning approach. As market conditions improved, the portfolio increased participation selectively, aligning with strengthening trends while continuing to prioritise risk control.

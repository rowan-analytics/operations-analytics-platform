## ⚖️ Risk Management

Risk management throughout the period was driven by a focus on **capital preservation, controlled exposure, and maintaining flexibility** in an increasingly reactive market environment. Maximum drawdown was contained to approximately **-4.9%**, reflecting proactive reduction of exposure during weaker market conditions and avoidance of deeper capital impairment during periods of unstable price action.

---

### 📉 Drawdown Control

Maximum drawdown remained contained, supported by deliberate reductions in exposure during low-conviction periods. This prevented deeper capital erosion during unstable phases and allowed the portfolio to maintain flexibility for re-engagement as conditions improved.

---

### ⚖️ Exposure & Beta Management

Portfolio exposure was actively managed, with an average beta of approximately **0.40** relative to Bitcoin. Lower directional exposure during the early phase limited downside participation, while increased effective exposure during improving conditions enabled stronger upside capture.

This dynamic adjustment of beta was a key contributor to both **drawdown control** and **overall performance**.

---

### 📊 Volatility Management

Volatility was managed through selective participation and position sizing, resulting in lower realised volatility during the initial phase of the period. As conditions stabilised, increased participation enabled more efficient capture of directional moves, improving overall risk-adjusted returns.

This reflects a preference for engaging in **higher-quality opportunities** rather than maintaining constant exposure.

---

### 💧 Liquidity & Optionality

A significant proportion of capital was held in cash throughout the period, providing both downside protection and strategic optionality. This allowed the portfolio to avoid forced participation during uncertain conditions and deploy capital more effectively as market structure improved.

Maintaining liquidity was a **deliberate component of the risk framework**, rather than a passive outcome.

---

### 🧠 Risk Framework

The portfolio’s risk approach is centred on **dynamic exposure management, asymmetric positioning, and capital preservation**, with the objective of limiting downside while retaining the flexibility to scale into higher-conviction opportunities.

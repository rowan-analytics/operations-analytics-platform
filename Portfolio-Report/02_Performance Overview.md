## 📊 Performance Overview

Portfolio performance was shaped by a **two-phase market environment**. The first half of the month was characterised by weaker and more volatile price action, during which exposure remained deliberately constrained. This limited downside participation and preserved capital through a less directionally reliable period.

As market conditions improved over the final two weeks, the portfolio captured a greater share of upside through selective exposure to strengthening trends. This shift in participation drove a recovery in cumulative returns and supported outperformance relative to Bitcoin over the full period.

---

### 📈 Equity Curve

The equity curve reflects a disciplined progression through changing market conditions. Early weakness was relatively contained, avoiding the deeper capital erosion often associated with full directional exposure in unstable environments. As trend quality improved later in the month, portfolio returns accelerated, resulting in a stronger end-of-period trajectory relative to the benchmark.

---

### 📉 Drawdown Profile

Drawdowns remained controlled throughout the period, with capital preservation supported by reduced early exposure and selective re-engagement as conditions improved. The drawdown profile suggests that downside risk was managed proactively rather than reactively, allowing the portfolio to retain flexibility and participate more effectively once the environment became more supportive.

---

### 📊 Rolling Sharpe

Rolling Sharpe was weaker during the early phase of the month, reflecting a noisier and less efficient return environment. As conditions stabilised and upside capture improved in the latter half of the period, rolling risk-adjusted performance strengthened, indicating that returns became more efficient rather than merely more directional.

---

### ⚖️ Benchmark Comparison

Relative to Bitcoin, the portfolio benefited from lower downside participation during the weaker early phase and stronger selective capture during the recovery. This resulted in excess return being driven primarily by exposure management and timing of participation, rather than broad passive market beta alone.

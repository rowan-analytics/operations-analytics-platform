# 📊 Portfolio Performance Report

### March – April 2026

---

## 📌 Executive Summary

The portfolio returned **+5.9% MTD**, outperforming **Bitcoin (+2.7%)** by **+3.2%**, while maintaining a contained **maximum drawdown of -4.3%**. Performance was generated in a market that remained constructive but increasingly selective, where returns were driven more by allocation discipline and relative-strength exposure than by broad passive beta.

Risk-adjusted performance remained strong across multiple measures, with a **Sharpe ratio of 1.68**, **Sortino ratio of 2.34**, **Calmar ratio of 1.37**, **Omega ratio of 1.29**, and an **Information ratio of 1.06**. A **beta of 0.74 to Bitcoin** indicates that returns were not solely driven by market direction, but by allocation quality and selective positioning.

The higher Sortino relative to Sharpe suggests that downside volatility was well controlled, while the Calmar ratio highlights efficient drawdown management. An Omega ratio above 1 reflects a favourable return distribution, and the Information ratio confirms that excess returns were generated consistently rather than opportunistically.

Performance was primarily driven by selective exposure to assets demonstrating strong relative strength and improving liquidity conditions, with allocation decisions contributing more to excess return than passive market exposure. Downside risk was managed through reduced exposure during lower-conviction periods, although rising cross-asset correlations limited diversification benefits toward the end of the period.

Market conditions remained highly reactive to macro and geopolitical developments, resulting in more **headline-driven price behaviour** and less stable trend continuation. This reinforced the importance of disciplined exposure management and selective participation.

Current positioning reflects a focus on **accumulating long-term high-conviction assets within a perceived value zone**, rather than maximising short-term directional exposure. Capital is deployed selectively while maintaining a **majority cash allocation** to preserve flexibility.

Looking ahead, the outlook remains constructive but selective. If market breadth and liquidity improve, risk can be increased incrementally. If momentum weakens and volatility rises without follow-through, exposure will be reduced in favour of capital preservation and relative value opportunities.
